REST isn't actually a word - it's an acronym. It comes from three words of equal importance:

- **==RE==**presentational
- **==S==**tate
- **==T==**ransfer

**RE** stands for _Representational_. It means that our machinery **stores, transmits and receives representations**

State: if any of the properties changes its value, this inevitably entails the effect of changing the whole object's state. Such a change is often called a **transition**.

**T** stands for _Transfer_. The network (not only the Internet) is able to act as a **carrier allowing you to transmit states' representations to and from the server**.
