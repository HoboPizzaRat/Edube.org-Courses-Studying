An end-point is **a point where the data is available to get it from and where the data may be sent to**.

We can say that all modern OSs implement BSD sockets in a more or less accurate way. Despite their differences, the general idea remains the same and this is what we are going to tell you about.

The main idea behind BSD sockets is closely connected to Unix philosophy contained in the words everything is a file.
- A socket may be often treated as very specific kind of file
- Writing to a socket results in sending the data through a network. Reading from a socket enables you to receive the data coming from the network.
